---
name: HERBORA educational scope
description: Product trust boundaries for the educational Ayurvedic herbal knowledge prototype.
---

HERBORA is an academic, research, and educational project, not a clinical authority. Do not claim herbs cure disease, diagnose, or prescribe. Do not add GPS or location tracking, blockchain, QR or supply-chain tracking, or user tracking.

**Why:** The product owner specified these boundaries in the project brief.

**How to apply:** Keep traditional context distinct from clinical evidence, show sources only when supplied, and do not introduce the excluded tracking features.
